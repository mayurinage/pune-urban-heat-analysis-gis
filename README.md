# Mapping Urban Heat Exposure and Vegetation Cooling Potential in Pune, India

## Project Overview
This GIS and Remote Sensing project investigates land surface temperature, vegetation distribution, and potential heat-priority areas within Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC), India.

## Objectives
- Map land surface temperature (LST) using Landsat 9 imagery.
- Assess vegetation distribution using NDVI.
- Identify areas combining high surface temperature and low vegetation.
- Highlight potential locations for urban greening and further investigation.

## Tools & Technologies
- Google Earth Engine
- QGIS
- Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level 2
- NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)
- GIS raster analysis and cartography

## Study Area
Pune Municipal Corporation, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

## Methodology
1. Processed Landsat 9 imagery from March 1 to June 1, 2026.
2. Applied cloud and shadow masking.
3. Derived Land Surface Temperature (LST) in Celsius.
4. Calculated NDVI to assess vegetation.
5. Identified heat-priority pixels using the 95th percentile of LST and the 25th percentile of NDVI.
6. Prepared final thematic maps in QGIS.

## Key Findings
- Mean Land Surface Temperature: 45.04°C
- Maximum Land Surface Temperature: 55.94°C
- Mean NDVI: 0.153
- Heat-priority area: 639.11 hectares
- Heat-priority area share: 2.54% of PMC

## Deliverables
- Land Surface Temperature map
- Vegetation (NDVI) map
- Heat-priority zones map
- Raster outputs
- QGIS project file

## Limitations
This is a satellite-based surface heat screening analysis, not an air-temperature or validated human heat-risk assessment. Results are based on a limited set of Landsat scenes and have not been ground-validated.
