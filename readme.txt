# Mapping Urban Heat Exposure and Vegetation Cooling Potential in Pune, India

**Project Type:** GIS & Remote Sensing  
**Study Area:** Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC), Pune, Maharashtra, India  
**Satellite Data:** Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level-2  
**Analysis Period:** March–May 2026  
**Spatial Resolution:** 30 metres

## Objective
To identify areas with high land surface temperature and low vegetation cover in Pune PMC using GIS and remote sensing techniques.

## Key Analysis
- Land Surface Temperature (LST) estimation
- Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) calculation
- Identification of high-heat areas
- Identification of low-vegetation areas
- Heat priority screening through spatial overlay

## Methodology
Landsat 9 imagery was processed using Google Earth Engine. LST and NDVI were derived from satellite bands. Statistical percentile thresholds were used to identify high-temperature and low-vegetation areas. The overlapping zones were mapped in QGIS.

## Key Finding
Approximately 639 hectares (2.54% of the PMC study area) were identified as heat-priority screening zones based on the overlap of the highest 5% LST and lowest 25% NDVI.

## Deliverables
- Land Surface Temperature Map
- NDVI Map
- Heat Priority Screening Map
- Processed raster datasets
- QGIS project file

## Limitation
This is a satellite-based surface heat screening analysis. It does not represent measured air temperature or independently validated human heat exposure.
